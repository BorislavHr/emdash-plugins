# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Posts an entry to Discord once, when it is first published; never corrections, republishing or
  imports.
- A switch under Plugins > Discord, off until an admin turns it on.
- A **Discord** panel in the entry editor: Post to Discord, Post again, Post an update, and
  Don't post this one.
- Failed posts are tried again every 15 minutes, up to five times; at most 30 posts an hour.
- Options for the collections, fields, taxonomy, pictures, colour, webhook variable and every text.
